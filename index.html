FAQ

Clear answers before you upgrade.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Boosting runs locally in the tab with the Web Audio API. We never receive your media stream.

Why is the checkout total higher than $3.99 / $29.99 / $79.99?

Those are base USD prices. Checkout may add tax (18% GST) so you see the full amount before you pay. INR prices use USD × FX rate (default 95.12), rounded — currently ₹380 / ₹2,853 / ₹7,609 with tax included.

Which payment methods are supported?

Pay with PayPal (USD), or Razorpay for cards and UPI. Card details stay with the provider. We never store them. Same pattern as Screen Time Tracker.

What happens when my plan ends?

Monthly and yearly Pro end on the expiry date. Lifetime does not expire. After expiry you return to Free limits.

Do I need a license key?

No. After payment, open Volume Booster and use the same billing email — Pro unlocks automatically.

Is Volume Booster related to FxSound?

No. Volume Booster is an original Fenwick Labs Chrome extension. We studied common volume-enhancer patterns only — no FxSound assets, branding, or code.
