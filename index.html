FAQ

Clear answers before you upgrade.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Boosting runs locally in the tab with the Web Audio API. We never receive your media stream.

Why can the checkout total differ from the plan price?

Applicable tax is calculated and shown before payment. INR totals are converted using the current checkout exchange rate.

Which payment methods are supported?

Pay with PayPal (USD), or Razorpay for cards and UPI. Card details stay with the provider. We never store them. Same pattern as Screen Time Tracker.

How do I use Pro after payment?

Open XCoda from your Chrome toolbar with the same account used at checkout. Your Pro controls will be ready there.

Is XCoda related to another audio product?

No. XCoda is an original Fenwick Labs Chrome extension with independently designed code, branding, and interface.
